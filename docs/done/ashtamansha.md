# ‌අෂ්ඨමාංශ

Need to go to each planet one by one.
Need to find the sign of 8th house of the planet in the birth chart.
Then need to find that sign's house in the navansaka chart.
If the planet in that house the planet can mark as the Astamansha planet.

As example,

1995-12-04 02:27 | Sri Jayawardenepura General Hospital

This birth chart's the Rahu in the 2nd house. Then 8th house of the Rahu is the 9th house of birth chart. It is වෘශභ sign. Now need to check වෘශභ sign's house in the Navansaka chart. The Rahu is not in that house. So Rahu is NOT Ashtamansha. (If Rahu is in that house so Rahu become Ashtamsnsha)
