**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Source:** `docs/rash_aspects.md`
**Status:** Draft
**Related:** `20260809-2133-planet-aspects.md` (Planet Aspects / දෘෂ්ඨි setting — this feature composes with it)

## Background / Context

The Planet Aspects feature (`20260809-2133-planet-aspects.md`) introduced a per-user setting that drives house/planet aspect calculation from configured per-planet aspect houses and degrees, using the per-user `planetaryOrbs` as the orb tolerance.

This feature introduces a second aspect mechanism, **Rashi Aspects** (රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි): sign-to-sign (rasi-to-rasi) drishti governed by the fixed **Chara / Thira / Ubaya** category rules. The rashi rules are the same for every student — the new setting only switches the mechanism on/off (and, optionally, per-sign opt-outs). When enabled, rashi drishti is an **additional source** of house and planet aspects, composed (unioned) with the Planet Aspects setting, and the aspect tooltip must show **every** reason for an aspect as its own line.

Rashi-aspect rules from the feature document (`docs/rash_aspects.md`):

- **Chara rashis** (movable — මේෂ Aries, කටක Cancer, තුලා Libra, මකර Capricorn = 1, 4, 7, 10) look at **Thira rashis** (fixed — වෘෂභ Taurus, සිංහ Leo, වෘශ්චික Scorpio, කුම්භ Aquarius = 2, 5, 8, 11) but NOT their nearest rashi. Example: මේෂ aspects සිංහ, වෘශ්චික, කුම්භ but NOT වෘෂභ.
- **Thira rashis** look at **Chara rashis** but NOT their nearest rashi. Example: වෘෂභ aspects කටක, තුලා, මකර but NOT මේෂ.
- **Ubaya rashis** (dual — මිථුන Gemini, කන්‍යා Virgo, ධනු Sagittarius, මීන Pisces = 3, 6, 9, 12) look at **other Ubaya rashis**. Example: මිථුන aspects කන්‍යා, ධනු.

How rashi aspects apply to houses and planets (from the feature document):

- A planet in a Chara rashi looks at the aspected Thira rashis → it aspects the **houses** of those rashis, and **planets** in those rashis are aspected.
- The planet's degree and its "rashmi" degree/orb are still used, as in other aspect calculations.

Tooltip requirements (from the feature document):

- The tooltip shows the reason for the aspect, e.g. `රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි මේෂ (+02:05:00)`.
- An aspect may have **multiple reasons** — all are shown as **multiple lines**, e.g.:
  ```
  ග්‍රහ දෘෂ්ඨි 7 (180) (+02:05:00)
  රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි මේෂ රාශිය මිථුනය දකී (+02:05:00)
  ```

**Clarification (2026-08-11):** rashi drishti applies to **BOTH** manually-entered horoscopes (`source: "manual"`) and birth-time/auto horoscopes (`source: "auto"`). Manual charts carry per-planet degrees (`ManualHousePlacements.planetDegrees`, see `data-model.md` and the companion `20260809-2133-planet-aspects.md` clarification) so the rashi candidate sets and the degree + orb ("rashmi") check run identically on both sources. US-RA-004 / US-RA-005 below are updated accordingly; Open Question 5 is resolved with the remaining degree-input items tracked in US-PA Open Questions.

## Rashi Aspects Actors

### Student (Settings Context)

- **Description**: An astrology student who studies horoscopes and customizes how aspects are computed.
- **Rashi-Setting-Specific Goals**: See and understand the rashi-aspect logic, enable/disable rashi drishti, and read every reason behind an aspect.
- **Rashi-Setting-Specific Permissions**:
    - Open the "Rashi Aspects" (රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි) setting and view its Chara/Thira/Ubaya rule summary
    - Enable/disable the setting for own calculations
    - Optionally disable rashi drishti for a specific aspecting sign (per-sign override — phase-gated)
    - See house/planet aspects recomputed to include rashi drishti when the setting is enabled — on both auto (birth-time) and manually-entered horoscopes
    - View all aspect reasons in the tooltip (planetary drishti line + one line per rashi drishti reason)
    - Reset the setting to its default (disabled)
    - Persist the setting for later sessions

### System (Calculation Engine)

- **Description**: Automated services that compute house aspects and planet aspects.
- **Rashi-Setting-Specific Responsibilities**:
    - Read the per-user `rashiAspects` setting and, when enabled, apply the fixed Chara/Thira/Ubaya rashi-aspect rules as an additional source of house and planet aspects
    - Apply the aspecting planet's degree and its `planetaryOrbs` value ("rashmi") as the orb tolerance, matching other aspect calculations — for both auto and manual horoscopes (manual planets use their stored or derived fallback degree)
    - Union rashi-drishti reasons with the Planet-Aspects-setting reasons for the same aspected house/planet, retaining each distinct reason
    - Emit enough per-aspect reason/source data for the UI to render each reason as its own tooltip line
    - Fall back to unchanged behavior (Planet Aspects setting only) when `rashiAspects.enabled` is false

### US-RA-001: Rashi-aspects setting visibility and configuration

- **Title**: Student views and enables the "Rashi Aspects" (රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි) setting
- **Description**: As a student, I want to view the Rashi Aspects setting and its Chara/Thira/Ubaya logic and enable it so that my house/planet aspects include rashi drishti when I study with it.
- **Priority**: High
- **Actor**: Student
- **Dependencies**: None (independent setting UI, rendered alongside the Planet Aspects setting)

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. **Setting Location**:
    - The settings area includes an entry labeled "Rashi Aspects" (රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි)
    - The setting is available to students in both Sinhala and English

2. **Informational Logic Display**:
    - The setting shows the rashi-aspect logic it applies: Chara rashis look at Thira rashis, Thira rashis look at Chara rashis, Ubaya rashis look at Ubaya rashis, and each rashi excludes its nearest rashi
    - The display is illustrative/informational — the rules themselves are not editable

3. **Enable/Disable Toggle**:
    - Given the Rashi Aspects setting, When the student toggles it ON, Then the choice is saved per user and rashi drishti is applied to subsequent house/planet aspect calculations
    - Given the setting enabled, When the student toggles it OFF, Then aspect calculation returns to the Planet-Aspects-setting-only behavior

4. **Persistence**:
    - Saving stores the configuration per user (survives logout/login and browser refresh)
    - The saved state is pre-filled on reopen for later editing

#### Edge Cases

- **First run (no configuration)**: The setting shows its default state (`enabled: false`) and does not block saving
- **Toggle without changes**: Saving the current state is a no-op with a confirmation message
- **Repeated toggling**: Each save persists the latest state; no accumulation of stale toggles

#### Business Rules

- The setting is stored per user on the `User` document as `rashiAspects` (`{ enabled: boolean, overrides: {} }`), following the existing `planetAspects`/`planetaryOrbs` settings pattern
- The rashi-aspect rules are fixed system rules and are NOT user-editable — only the on/off state (and optionally per-sign overrides) is configurable

### US-RA-002: Chara/Thira/Ubaya rashi-aspect rules encoded

- **Title**: System encodes the Chara/Thira/Ubaya rashi-aspect category rules
- **Description**: As a student, I want the system to apply the fixed category rules — Chara rashis aspect Thira rashis, Thira rashis aspect Chara rashis, Ubaya rashis aspect Ubaya rashis — so that rashi drishti reflects the documented logic.
- **Priority**: High
- **Actor**: System
- **Dependencies**: US-RA-001

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. **Chara → Thira**:
    - Given a planet in a Chara rashi (1, 4, 7, 10), When rashi aspects are applied, Then its candidate aspected signs are the Thira rashis (2, 5, 8, 11), reduced by the nearest-rashi exclusion (US-RA-003)

2. **Thira → Chara**:
    - Given a planet in a Thira rashi (2, 5, 8, 11), When rashi aspects are applied, Then its candidate aspected signs are the Chara rashis (1, 4, 7, 10), reduced by the nearest-rashi exclusion (US-RA-003)

3. **Ubaya → Ubaya**:
    - Given a planet in an Ubaya rashi (3, 6, 9, 12), When rashi aspects are applied, Then its candidate aspected signs are the other Ubaya rashis (3, 6, 9, 12), reduced by the nearest-rashi exclusion (US-RA-003)

4. **Fixed Category Mapping**:
    - Given any of the 12 rashis, When the category is looked up, Then each sign belongs to exactly one category: Chara {1, 4, 7, 10}, Thira {2, 5, 8, 11}, Ubaya {3, 6, 9, 12} (see the Rashi Category table in `data-model.md`), and this mapping never varies per student

#### Edge Cases

- **Zodiac wrap (12 → 1)**: Category membership and candidate derivation work across the 12/1 boundary (e.g. මීන Pisces 12 and මේෂ Aries 1)
- **No sign in two categories**: Every sign resolves to exactly one category; no overlap or gap across the 12 signs
- **Empty aspected set**: A sign always has at least 2 aspected signs, so the aspected set is never empty

#### Business Rules

- The Chara/Thira/Ubaya mapping is the authoritative fixed table in `data-model.md` (Rashi Category)
- Signs are referenced by their numeric ZodiacSign enum values (1–12), never display strings
- Category derivation and candidate derivation are deterministic and pure — no ephemeris dependency

### US-RA-003: Nearest-rashi exclusion rule

- **Title**: System excludes the nearest rashi from a rashi's aspected targets
- **Description**: As a student, I want the nearest-rashi exclusion applied precisely so that e.g. මේෂ Aries aspects සිංහ Leo, වෘශ්චික Scorpio and කුම්භ Aquarius but NOT වෘෂභ Taurus.
- **Priority**: High
- **Actor**: System
- **Dependencies**: US-RA-002

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. **Aries excludes Taurus**:
    - Given a planet in මේෂ Aries (1), When its aspected-sign set is derived, Then it is {5, 8, 11} — සිංහ Leo, වෘශ්චික Scorpio, කුම්භ Aquarius — and වෘෂභ Taurus (2) is NOT included

2. **Taurus excludes Aries**:
    - Given a planet in වෘෂභ Taurus (2), When its aspected-sign set is derived, Then it is {4, 7, 10} — කටක Cancer, තුලා Libra, මකර Capricorn — and මේෂ Aries (1) is NOT included

3. **Gemini excludes Pisces**:
    - Given a planet in මිථුන Gemini (3), When its aspected-sign set is derived, Then it is {6, 9} — කන්‍යා Virgo, ධනු Sagittarius — and මීන Pisces (12) is NOT included (the Ubaya nearest-rashi tie is resolved by excluding the higher sign number)

4. **Full 12-row lookup**:
    - Given every rashi as the aspecting sign, When the aspected-sign set is derived, Then it matches the authoritative table in `data-model.md` (Rashi Aspects Rules) — including කටක Cancer → {2, 8, 11}, තුලා Libra → {2, 5, 11}, මකර Capricorn → {2, 5, 8}, සිංහ Leo → {1, 7, 10}, වෘශ්චික Scorpio → {1, 4, 10}, කුම්භ Aquarius → {1, 4, 7}

#### Edge Cases

- **Adjacent sign of another category**: e.g. මිථුන Gemini's immediate neighbours (වෘෂභ Taurus, කටක Cancer) are NOT Ubaya, so the exclusion for Gemini comes solely from the arc-distance tie between කන්‍යා Virgo and මීන Pisces — never from adjacency
- **Equidistant target signs (Ubaya tie)**: The exclusion is resolved deterministically — among the two nearest Ubaya signs (arc distance 3), the one with the **higher sign number** (closer to Pisces/12) is excluded
- **Determinism**: The excluded sign is always uniquely determined; there is no randomness or ambiguity in the output
- **Self-exclusion**: A sign never aspects itself (it is never a target of its own category rule)

#### Business Rules

- "Nearest" = minimum zodiacal arc distance (minor arc, 1–12 sign space)
- Chara/Thira exclusions always coincide with the unique adjacent sign of the target category (the two categories strictly alternate around the zodiac)
- Ubaya exclusions use the arc-distance tie-break: exclude the higher sign number of the two nearest targets
- The derivation is deterministic and pure; the 12-row lookup table is the single source of truth (see `data-model.md`)

### US-RA-004: Rashi aspects applied to house aspects

- **Title**: System applies rashi aspects to house aspects
- **Description**: As a student, I want a planet in a sign to aspect the houses of the rashis it rashi-aspects so that house aspects include rashi drishti when the setting is enabled.
- **Priority**: High
- **Actor**: System
- **Dependencies**: US-RA-002, US-RA-003

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. **Houses of the Aspected Rashis**:
    - Given a planet in මේෂ Aries (1) with aspected-sign set {5, 8, 11}, When house aspects are computed with Rashi Aspects enabled, Then every house whose whole-sign `sign` is 5 (සිංහ Leo), 8 (වෘශ්චික Scorpio) or 11 (කුම්භ Aquarius) is aspected by that planet

2. **Degree + Orb Still Apply**:
    - Given a candidate house (one of the aspected rashis' houses) within the aspecting planet's orb, When the aspect is recorded, Then it is effective only when the house middle degree is within the aspecting planet's `planetaryOrbs` value ("rashmi") of the relevant aspect point derived from the aspecting planet's degree — matching other aspect calculations
    - Given a candidate house outside the aspecting planet's orb, When evaluated, Then no rashi-drishti house aspect is recorded for it

3. **Union with the Planet Aspects Setting**:
    - Given a house aspected both via rashi drishti and via the Planet Aspects setting (US-PA-005), When house aspects are computed, Then the house is aspected once and **both** reasons are retained (rendered as separate tooltip lines, US-RA-006)

4. **House Sign Not in Aspected Set**:
    - Given a house whose whole-sign `sign` is NOT in the aspecting planet's aspected-sign set, When evaluated, Then the house is NOT aspected via rashi drishti (it may still be aspected via the Planet Aspects setting)

#### Edge Cases

- **Aspect-free houses**: Houses whose signs are outside the aspected-sign set show no rashi-drishti aspect (Planet-Aspects-setting aspects may still apply)
- **Placidus vs whole-sign**: For auto charts, the whole-sign (Rasi) `sign` of the house determines the rashi-drishti candidate set; unequal-house cusp details (middle sign/degree) feed the degree+orb check exactly as in the Planet Aspects degree arm
- **Both chart sources (clarified 2026-08-11)**: Rashi house aspects apply to `source: "auto"` AND `source: "manual"` horoscopes. On manual charts the whole-sign house `sign` supplies the candidate set (already stored per house), and the degree+orb check uses each planet's stored or fallback-derived degree (`ManualHousePlacements.planetDegrees`) against the house's whole-sign sign midpoint `(sign−1)*30+15` — the manual house-middle reference (see `data-model.md`)
- **Planet's own house**: A planet's own sign/house is only aspected if it is in the aspected-sign set (which, by the rules, never contains the planet's own sign)

#### Business Rules

- Rashi drishti is an **additional (union) source** of house aspects; it does not replace the Planet Aspects setting
- The whole-sign (Rasi) sign of the house determines whether it is a rashi-drishti candidate
- Degree + orb ("rashmi") always govern effectiveness, using the aspecting planet's `planetaryOrbs` value
- Derivation is deterministic and pure
- **Both chart sources (clarified 2026-08-11)**: rashi house aspects apply to `source: "auto"` and `source: "manual"` horoscopes; on manual charts the degree+orb check uses each planet's stored or fallback-derived degree (`ManualHousePlacements.planetDegrees`)

### US-RA-005: Rashi aspects applied to planet aspects

- **Title**: System applies rashi aspects to planet aspects
- **Description**: As a student, I want a planet to aspect other planets in the rashis it rashi-aspects so that planet-to-planet aspects include rashi drishti when the setting is enabled.
- **Priority**: High
- **Actor**: System
- **Dependencies**: US-RA-002, US-RA-003

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. **Planets in the Aspected Rashis**:
    - Given a planet in මේෂ Aries (1) with aspected-sign set {5, 8, 11}, When planet aspects are computed with Rashi Aspects enabled, Then every other planet whose `sign` is 5 (සිංහ Leo), 8 (වෘශ්චික Scorpio) or 11 (කුම්භ Aquarius) is a rashi-drishti candidate for aspecting

2. **Degree + Orb Still Apply**:
    - Given a candidate planet within the aspecting planet's orb of the relevant aspect point, When recorded, Then the matched angle (`aspectType`/`exactAspectDegree`) and `degreeGap` are computed exactly as in the standard planet-aspect calculation, using the aspecting planet's `planetaryOrbs` value as the orb tolerance
    - Given a candidate planet outside the orb of every relevant aspect point, When evaluated, Then no rashi-drishti planet aspect is recorded

3. **Union with the Planet Aspects Setting**:
    - Given a target planet aspected both via rashi drishti and via the configured degrees (US-PA-006), When planet aspects are computed, Then the aspect is recorded once and **both** reasons are retained (US-RA-006)

4. **Conjunction Unchanged**:
    - Co-located planets (conjunction) continue to be handled as today; rashi drishti is not required for a conjunction to exist

#### Edge Cases

- **Same-sign planets**: A planet never rashi-aspects another planet in its own sign (its own sign is never in its aspected-sign set)
- **Planets in non-aspected signs**: Never receive a rashi-drishti reason (they may still be aspected via the Planet Aspects setting)
- **Retrograde/combustion**: Independent of the rashi rule — they neither add nor remove a rashi-drishti aspect
- **Multiple aspected-sign targets**: A planet in a sign with several aspected signs yields separate candidates per target sign, each evaluated with the degree + orb check
- **Both chart sources (clarified 2026-08-11)**: rashi planet aspects apply to `source: "manual"` horoscopes too — the target planet's `sign` supplies the candidate set (already known from its house), and the aspecting planet's stored or fallback-derived degree (`ManualHousePlacements.planetDegrees`) drives the degree+orb check, matching the auto path

#### Business Rules

- Rashi drishti is an **additional (union) source** of planet aspects; it does not replace the Planet Aspects setting (US-PA-006)
- The target's `sign` (numeric ZodiacSign enum) determines whether it is a rashi-drishti candidate
- Degree + orb ("rashmi") always govern effectiveness, using the aspecting planet's `planetaryOrbs` value
- Derivation is deterministic and pure
- **Both chart sources (clarified 2026-08-11)**: rashi planet aspects are computed for `source: "auto"` and `source: "manual"` horoscopes alike

### US-RA-006: Two-line tooltip reasons

- **Title**: Aspect tooltip shows all reasons for an aspect as multiple lines
- **Description**: As a student, I want the aspect tooltip to show every reason for an aspect on its own line so that I understand why the aspect exists (e.g. both the planetary drishti line and the rashi drishti line).
- **Priority**: High
- **Actor**: System (data) / Student (display)
- **Dependencies**: US-RA-004, US-RA-005

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. **Multiple Reason Lines**:
    - Given an aspect with both a planetary-drishti reason and a rashi-drishti reason, When the tooltip renders, Then each reason is shown as its own line, exactly as documented:
        ```
        ග්‍රහ දෘෂ්ඨි 7 (180) (+02:05:00)
        රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි මේෂ රාශිය මිථුනය දකී (+02:05:00)
        ```
    - (EN locale renders the same two lines with the EN phrasing, e.g. `Planet drishti 7 (180) (+02:05:00)` and the EN rashi-drishti line)

2. **Single Reason Stays a Single Line**:
    - Given an aspect with only one reason (e.g. planetary drishti only), When the tooltip renders, Then exactly one line is shown — the existing single-line behavior (UX §8.1.1) is preserved for single-reason aspects

3. **Reason Source Available**:
    - Given a rashi-drishti aspect, When the aspect data is returned, Then the response carries enough per-aspect reason/source data for the UI to distinguish the rashi-drishti line from the planetary-drishti line and render each on its own line (e.g. a per-entry `reason: "planetary" | "rashi"` marker or a `reasons[]` list — shape to be decided in the Architecture phase)

4. **Both Locales**:
    - Given the tooltip renders, When the locale is Sinhala or English, Then the reason lines render in the active locale (numeric values are locale-neutral)

#### Edge Cases

- **Three or more reasons**: If an aspect has more than two reasons (e.g. planetary + two rashi-drishti contributions), all reason lines render, one per line
- **Conjunction/legacy aspects**: The planetary line omits the house token as today; the rashi-drishti line (when present) follows the same convention
- **Tooltip overflow**: Multi-line content stays within the tooltip's max-width with wrapping; long Sinhala lines are not clipped
- **Reason ordering**: The planetary-drishti line renders before rashi-drishti line(s)

#### Business Rules

- Reason lines are composed in the UI from numeric fields + i18n keys — never stored as text (repo convention)
- Each distinct reason appears exactly once; the same reason is never duplicated
- The tooltip remains the sole in-table surface for aspect reason detail (chips show the aspecting body's name only)

### US-RA-007: Defaults / reset (optional scope)

- **Title**: Student resets the Rashi Aspects setting to its default
- **Description**: As a student, I want to reset the Rashi Aspects setting back to its default (disabled) so that I can undo my choice.
- **Priority**: Medium (optional — confirm with PM/domain whether the toggle alone suffices)
- **Actor**: Student
- **Dependencies**: US-RA-001

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. **Reset to Default**:
    - Given the Rashi Aspects setting enabled (with any per-sign overrides), When the student resets it, Then `enabled` returns to `false`, any per-sign overrides are cleared, and aspect calculation returns to the Planet-Aspects-setting-only behavior

2. **Reset Confirmation**:
    - The reset requires confirmation before applying (destructive action)

3. **Effect on Calculation**:
    - After reset, no rashi-drishti house/planet aspects are produced until the setting is re-enabled

#### Edge Cases

- **Already-default state**: Reset is a no-op with a confirmation message ("Rashi Aspects already uses the default settings")
- **Bulk reset vs toggle**: A single reset action covers both the enabled flag and all overrides

#### Business Rules

- Default `rashiAspects` = `{ enabled: false, overrides: {} }`
- A reset Rashi Aspects setting behaves exactly like a never-configured one

## Validation Rules Summary

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Setting field | `rashiAspects.enabled` — Boolean master switch on the `User` document |
| Overrides field | `rashiAspects.overrides` — optional Record keyed by ZodiacSign enum string `"1"`…`"12"`, each `{ enabled: false }`; empty object = no overrides |
| Default | `{ enabled: false, overrides: {} }` — aspect calculation unchanged until enabled |
| Category mapping | Fixed: Chara {1, 4, 7, 10}, Thira {2, 5, 8, 11}, Ubaya {3, 6, 9, 12} (not configurable) |
| Chara → Thira | Aspects every Thira sign except the adjacent one (nearest exclusion) |
| Thira → Chara | Aspects every Chara sign except the adjacent one (nearest exclusion) |
| Ubaya → Ubaya | Aspects the other Ubaya signs excluding the nearest; two-way nearest tie resolved by excluding the higher sign number |
| House aspects | A planet aspects the houses whose whole-sign `sign` is in its aspected-sign set; union with the Planet Aspects setting; applies to both auto and manual charts |
| Planet aspects | A planet aspects planets whose `sign` is in its aspected-sign set; union with the Planet Aspects setting; applies to both auto and manual charts |
| Degree + orb | Effectiveness always governed by the aspecting planet's degree and its `planetaryOrbs` value ("rashmi") — matching other aspect calculations; manual charts use the stored or fallback-derived per-planet degree |
| Tooltip | Each distinct reason for an aspect renders as its own line (planetary first, then rashi); never stored as text |
| Recalculation | A setting change affects subsequent aspect calculations; no migration of stored `CalculatedDetails` (see Open Questions) |

## Clarifying Assumptions

1. **Per-user setting**: `rashiAspects` is a per-user setting stored on the `User` document, mirroring the `planetAspects`/`planetaryOrbs` pattern. Each student's charts are calculated with their own setting state.
2. **Rules are fixed system rules**: The Chara/Thira/Ubaya mapping and the nearest-rashi exclusion are the same for every student and are NOT user-editable. The setting only switches the mechanism on/off (plus optional per-sign overrides).
3. **Composition, not replacement**: Rashi drishti is an additional (union) source of house/planet aspects. It does not replace or alter the Planet Aspects setting — a house/planet may be aspected for both reasons, and both reasons are shown in the tooltip.
4. **Nearest-rashi rule**: "Nearest" is the minimum zodiacal arc distance. Chara/Thira exclusions coincide with the unique adjacent sign of the target category; Ubaya exclusions resolve the two-way nearest tie by excluding the higher sign number (so මිථුන Gemini → {කන්‍යා Virgo, ධනු Sagittarius}, excluding මීන Pisces). This matches all three examples in `docs/rash_aspects.md` and the task's verification set (Aries excludes Taurus; Taurus excludes Aries; Gemini excludes Pisces). The tie-break outcome for the other Ubaya aspecting signs is NOT pinned by the source doc — flagged in Open Questions.
5. **Degree + orb govern effectiveness**: Rashi drishti determines the candidate target signs; the aspect is actually recorded only when the target body/house-middle degree is within the aspecting planet's orb ("rashmi", its `planetaryOrbs` value) of the relevant aspect point — as in other aspect calculations.
6. **Both chart sources (clarified 2026-08-11)**: Rashi drishti applies to both `source: "auto"` and `source: "manual"` horoscopes. Manual charts use each planet's stored or fallback-derived degree (`ManualHousePlacements.planetDegrees`) for the degree+orb check, and the whole-sign house/planet `sign` for the candidate sets; no aspect behavior is exclusive to one chart source.
7. **Tooltip is multi-line-capable**: The existing single compact line (UX §8.1.1) becomes one line of a possible multi-line tooltip; single-reason aspects still show exactly one line. The UX doc (§8.1.1) needs a follow-up revision to document the multi-line rendering (flagged for UX).
8. **No migration**: Changing `rashiAspects` affects subsequent calculations; previously stored `CalculatedDetails` are not migrated (mirrors the Planet Aspects decision).

## Open Questions

1. **Integration with `planetAspects`**: How do the two settings combine at the data level? Does each aspect entry gain a `reason: "planetary" | "rashi"` marker, a `reasons[]` list, or are rashi aspects kept as a separate parallel collection keyed by target house/planet? Architect to decide; the tooltip needs a stable way to render each reason line.
2. **Ubaya tie-break confirmation**: `docs/rash_aspects.md` only pins the මිථුන Gemini example (aspects කන්‍යා Virgo, ධනු Sagittarius — excluding මීන Pisces). The tie-break for කන්‍යා Virgo, ධනු Sagittarius and මීන Pisces as *aspecting* signs is derived (exclude the higher sign number), not stated in the source. PM/domain to confirm the full Ubaya lookup.
3. **Source-doc example inconsistency**: The two-line tooltip example reads `රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි මේෂ රාශිය මිථුනය දකී` ("Mesha rashi looks at Mithuna") — but මේෂ Aries (Chara) does NOT rashi-aspect මිථුන Gemini (Ubaya) per the rules. The example is presumably illustrative; confirm the intended rashi-drishti line template and its content (signs, delta) so the example in the spec can be corrected.
4. **Short vs long rashi-drishti line**: The doc shows two formats — `රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි මේෂ (+02:05:00)` and `රාශි දෘෂ්ඨි මේෂ රාශිය මිථුනය දකී (+02:05:00)`. Which is the canonical tooltip line, or does the long form apply only in the two-line case? UX/PM to decide.
5. **RESOLVED (2026-08-11)**: Rashi aspects DO apply to manual horoscopes. Manual charts carry per-planet degrees (`ManualHousePlacements.planetDegrees`) and fall back to a deterministic derived degree (navamsa segment midpoint → sign midpoint 15°) for planets without an entered degree, so the same degree + orb ("rashmi") check runs on both sources. Open items on the manual-degree input UX (required vs optional, behavior of existing stored charts, golden data) are tracked in US-PA Open Questions 7.
6. **Re-derivation vs migration**: When `rashiAspects.enabled` changes, are previously saved `CalculatedDetails` re-derived (owner view only, like the Planet Aspects GET override) or only future calculations affected?
7. **Default state**: Should `enabled` default to `false` (opt-in, current behavior preserved) as assumed here, or `true` (opt-out) to match the doc's "modify all house and planet aspects using this" intent? PM/domain to decide.
8. **Per-sign overrides scope**: Is the optional `overrides` map (disable rashi drishti for a specific aspecting sign) needed at all in v1, or is the master toggle sufficient? PM/domain to decide.
9. **Ordering of rashi-drishti reasons**: When several reasons apply (e.g. multiple aspected-sign candidates for the same target), is the rashi-drishti line one per target sign or one aggregated line? UX/Developer to confirm.
